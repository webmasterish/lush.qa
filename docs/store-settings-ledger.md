Mirror benchmark: `lush.sa.com`. Not everything mirrors — payments, shipping, taxes and legal text are Qatar-specific.

Status: `done` · `todo` · `blocked` · `n/a`

**When changing a setting that already had a value, record the previous value in the row.** Surface C has no undo, no history and no git — the ledger line is the only way back.

## Incidents

### 2026-08-30, delivery charge missing on live orders

Kyaw reported an order that took no QAR 20 delivery charge; Dee forwarded it the same morning. Cause: eleven products had **"This is a physical product" unticked**, so they required no shipping. Where every line in an order was one of those, Shopify skipped the delivery step, charged nothing, and collected no delivery address. The missing address was a symptom, not the cause, and the shipping rates themselves were never at fault.

Verified against the full catalogue via the Admin API on the day: 551 products, 788 variants, exactly eleven affected and no others. Scrubee (7188), Ghostie (4978), Yoshi Egg (65195), Lord Of Misrule (65254), Pumpkin Scrubee (65258), Pumpkin Spice (65311), Monster Scare Bear (65261), Toffee Apple (65262), Swirly Pumpkin (65253), Mooncake (849), Snowy Mooncake (63955).

Cost: orders **#4213** and **#4217**, QAR 170 each, QAR 20 each not charged, QAR 40 total. Orders #4208 (QAR 610) and #4214 (QAR 310) held the same products but sat above the free-delivery threshold, so nothing was lost there. The client's two test orders that morning, #4218 and #4219, reproduced it cleanly.

Dee's team fixed ten of the eleven on 2026-08-30 between 10:06 and 10:10. **Outstanding: Pumpkin Spice, SKU 65311, still not a physical product**, active and published at `lush.qa/products/pumpkin-spice`. Flagged to Dee by email the same day, and left with the client at their request rather than fixed by us.

Two things worth carrying forward. The migration tool never sets `requiresShipping` (`migration-tool/src/entities/products.js`, `inventoryFields()` writes only `tracked` and `measurement`), so migrated variants take Shopify's default of true and the flag was unticked in the admin afterwards. And Shopify's product events do not log field-level changes, so there is no audit trail for who changed it or when. Anything depending on that flag has to be checked by querying it, not by reading history.
