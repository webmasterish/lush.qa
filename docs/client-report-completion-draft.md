## Lush.qa Migration to Shopify: Project Completion Report

Hi Dee,

lush.qa has been live on Shopify since Thursday 27 August 12:40, and everything is running as it should. 11 customer orders have come through in the first 72 hours, both languages are serving, the redirects from the old site are working, and your team was already adding products and putting up the Halloween slider within hours of going live. That is exactly how a launch should look.

This is the closing report on the project.

### Launch

- Sibin confirmed the nameservers change on 27 August after following up with Lush UK
- Mario gave us the green light to launch
- lush.qa, www.lush.qa, and account.lush.qa connected to Shopify, with security certificates issued for each
- Both languages, the redirects and the checkout were checked end-to-end.
- Store password removed at 12:40, and the first customer order arrived at 13:36, less than 1 hour after we made the store public.

### What was delivered

**Data.** Migrated with our own tooling and reconciled record by record against your old site, as set out in the data migration report of 20 July:

- 61 of 61 product categories, now Shopify collections
- 537 of 538 products, including images, prices, variants, stock levels and SEO details
- 1,915 customer accounts, plus guest purchases grouped into their own customer records for the first time
- 3,179 orders, keeping their original numbers, dates, items, totals and statuses

The content reflects your old site as of 3 August, which is when it became unreachable. Anything added after that date needs entering directly into Shopify.

**The store:**

- Be Yours theme, built to match the Lush Saudi store, including your catalogue, navigation and content
- English and Arabic throughout, including the checkout
- Homepage, navigation across 7 sections with drop down menus, collection filtering and sorting
- Product pages with ingredients, key ingredient cards, how to use and storage sections
- Product labels, the ingredients library, the WhatsApp button, the branches page, blog and policy pages
- 457 redirects, so links from Google, bookmarks and old newsletters still land on the right page
- Shipping set to free delivery over QAR 300 and QAR 20 below, tax switched off as confirmed, checkout and notification emails configured
- A Cloudflare account created under Al Mana's own mailbox and handed to Sibin, which also has room for your other domains at no extra cost
